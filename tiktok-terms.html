The service

viede-dev-stats is an application published by TecKnologikS. It lets the owner of a TikTok account schedule and publish their own videos to that account and read the statistics of their videos, through the TikTok for Developers APIs. It is an internal tool, used only for TikTok accounts owned and managed by TecKnologikS.

Use

By authorizing the application on TikTok, the account owner allows it to act within the permissions they grant. The account owner chooses every video, caption, privacy setting and publication time. They remain responsible for the content they publish and must comply with the TikTok Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

Personal data

How data is handled is described in the privacy policy.

Ending access

The account owner can revoke the application's access at any time from the TikTok app or by writing to support@tecknologiks.com.
