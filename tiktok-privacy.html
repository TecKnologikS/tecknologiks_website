Privacy policy — viede-dev-stats

Last updated: November 25, 2026.

Who we are

viede-dev-stats is an application published by TecKnologikS. It connects to TikTok through the TikTok for Developers APIs (Login Kit and Content Posting API). Contact: support@tecknologiks.com.

Who uses the application

The application is an internal tool. It is only used by TecKnologikS to publish videos to, and read statistics of, TikTok accounts that TecKnologikS owns and manages (for example the "Vie de Dev" account). It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

When an account owner authorizes the application on TikTok, and only within the permissions they grant, the application can access:

— basic profile information (open id, display name, avatar) and profile details (profile link, bio, verification status);
— account statistics (followers, following, likes, video count);
— the list of the account's videos and their statistics (views, likes, comments, shares);
— the access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok.

The application also sends to TikTok the videos, captions and settings that the account owner chooses to publish.

How we use it

Only to publish the account owner's own videos to their TikTok account, at the time they choose, and to show them the performance of their videos. We do not use TikTok data for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored on servers and devices controlled by TecKnologikS. It is not sold, rented or shared with third parties, except TikTok itself when publishing.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept as long as the account owner keeps the application authorized. The owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions), or by writing to support@tecknologiks.com: the tokens and the data collected for that account are then deleted.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new date.
